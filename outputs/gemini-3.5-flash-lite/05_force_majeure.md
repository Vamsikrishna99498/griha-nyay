## 🤖 Griha Nyay
**Your case in short**
Your possession is delayed by 1507 days (~49.5 months) past the promised date.

**Where you stand**
The law supports your right to claim relief for this delay. Under [RERA-18-1] "interest for every month of delay, till the handing over of the possession", if the builder fails to give possession by the agreed date, you are entitled to monthly interest. Regarding the builder's excuses, [RERA-6-FM] "war, flood, drought, fire, cyclone, earthquake or any other calamity caused by nature" defines valid force majeure, and whether the builder's reasons count is for TG-RERA to decide. You can check the TG-RERA portal to see if the authority granted them any official extension.

**Your two choices**
(a) Withdraw from the project and get your refund with interest and compensation [RERA-18-1]; the exact refund interest is unknown because payment dates were not provided. 
(b) Stay in the project and receive delay interest [RERA-18-1]. By my calculation, the interest accrued so far is Rs 24,86,550 (at an interest rate of 10.95% per year, calculated as the highest SBI Marginal Cost of Lending Rate plus 2% per year [TS-R15]), with a monthly interest of Rs 50,187, assuming payments were made on time. 
Since your goal is to get the flat, choice (b) fits your plan.

**What to do next**
1. Collect your payment receipts and agreement copies to confirm exact dates.
2. Check the project's registered details and extension status on the TG-RERA portal [FAQ-3].
3. Send a dated written notice to the builder demanding your delay interest under [FAQ-8] "interest payment is in accordance with the agreement and hence should be automatically paid", keeping proof of delivery.
4. If the builder fails to pay, file a complaint [TS-R34-1] [TS-R35-1].

**What I'm not sure about**
I am not sure of your exact agreement date or total flat price, as those details were missing. My calculations assume all payments were made before the promised date and use a simple interest estimate. The final rate, period, and any extension of time will be decided by the authority.
