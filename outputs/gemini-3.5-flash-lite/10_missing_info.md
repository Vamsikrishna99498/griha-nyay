**Where you stand**
The law supports your right to relief because the builder failed to hand over possession by the date in your agreement. If a builder cannot give possession as promised, you have the right to demand relief [RERA-18-1] "interest for every month of delay, till the handing over of the possession". Furthermore, [RERA-19-4] states that "The allottee shall be entitled to claim the refund of amount paid along with interest at such rate as may be prescribed".

**Your two choices**
(a) Withdraw and take a refund: You can ask for your money back with interest and compensation. The total refund amount so far is unknown because exact payment dates are missing. 
(b) Stay in the project: Since your goal is to get possession, you can stay and claim monthly delay interest. Based on your payment of Rs 50,00,000, the interest accrued so far is Rs 12,55,500, with a monthly interest of Rs 45,625 calculated at the rate of 10.95% per year [TS-R15]. To decide between these choices, ask yourself: do you still need this flat, or do you trust the builder to finish it?

4. If the builder does not respond, file a complaint. Complaints to the Authority are made using Form 'M' and claims for compensation go to the adjudicating officer in Form 'N', both with a fee of Rs 1,000 [TS-R34-1] [TS-R35-1].
