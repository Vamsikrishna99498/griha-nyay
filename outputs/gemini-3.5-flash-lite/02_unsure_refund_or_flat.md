Builder Greenvale Homes, project Greenvale Meadows in Tellapur. Possession was due June 2024. I paid 35 lakh so far. I don't know whether to ask for refund or wait, I really need a house for my family and rent is killing me. What should I do?

## 🤖 Griha Nyay
**Your case in short**
Your possession is delayed by 868 days (~28.5 months) since the promised date of 30 June 2024.

**Where you stand**
The law protects you when a builder fails to deliver on time. Under [RERA-18-1], "If the promoter fails to complete or is unable to give possession of an apartment, plot or building,- (a) in accordance with the terms of the agreement for sale", you have clear rights. The interest rate in Telangana is governed by [TS-R15], which sets it as the "highest Marginal Cost of Lending Rate of State Bank of India plus two per cent".

**Your two choices**
(a) Withdraw from the project to get your money back with interest and compensation, though the exact refund interest is unknown because payment dates were not provided. (b) Stay in the project and receive interest for every month of delay until handover. By my calculation, the interest accrued so far is Rs 9,11,400, and the monthly interest is Rs 31,937 based on Rs 35,00,000 paid. Since you need a house urgently and rent is high, ask yourself: do you still trust this builder to finish the project soon, or would you rather take your money and buy elsewhere?

**What to do next**
1. Collect your payment receipts, bank statements, and agreement papers.
2. Look up the builder's registered details on the TG-RERA portal.
3. Send a dated written notice to the builder and keep safe proof of delivery.
4. File a complaint. As noted in [TS-R34-1] and [TS-R35-1], complaints can be filed with the Authority in Form 'M' or claims can go to the adjudicating officer in Form 'N', with a fee of Rs 1,000.

**What I'm not sure about**
I calculated based on the assumption that your promised possession date was 30 June 2024 because only the month was given. I do not know your exact payment dates, so refund interest is unknown. The authority decides the final rate, period, and compensation. 

**When to get a real lawyer**
I am not a lawyer and I am not your lawyer. Consult a lawyer or free legal aid before you send or file anything, especially if you need help drafting your legal notice.
