**Your case in short**
The delay is 684 days (~22.5 months) past your promised possession date.

**Where you stand**
The law supports your right to seek relief for delayed possession. Under RERA, [RERA-18-1] "If the promoter fails to complete or is unable to give possession of an apartment, plot or building,- (a) in accordance with the terms of the agreement for sale or, as the case may be, duly completed by the date specified therein". Furthermore, [RERA-19-4] states that the "allottee shall be entitled to claim the refund of amount paid along with interest at such rate as may be prescribed and compensation in the manner as provided under this Act, from the promoter".

**Your two choices**
(a) Withdraw and get your money back with interest and compensation. My calculation of refund interest so far is unknown because individual payment dates are needed. 
(b) Stay in the project and get interest for every month of delay until handover. Based on your amount paid of Rs 40,00,000 at an interest rate of 10.95% per year, the interest accrued so far is Rs 8,20,800, with a monthly interest of Rs 36,500. 
Since your goal is a refund, choice (a) fits what you want.

**What to do next**
1. Collect proof: Gather your payment receipts, bank statements, and builder correspondence (payment dates are still lacking for precise calculations).
2. Check the project's registration on the TG-RERA portal as mentioned in [FAQ-3].
3. Send a dated written notice to the builder demanding your refund and keep proof of delivery.
4. File a complaint. As noted in the practice note for [TS-R34-1], refund and delay-interest complaints are decided by the Authority (Form M) and compensation by the adjudicating officer (Form N).

**What I'm not sure about**
I am not sure about exact refund interest because payment dates are missing. Also, my calculation assumes that only the month was given for the promised possession date, so the delay is counted from the last day of that month (31 December 2024). Furthermore, the final rate, period, and compensation are decided by TG-RERA.
