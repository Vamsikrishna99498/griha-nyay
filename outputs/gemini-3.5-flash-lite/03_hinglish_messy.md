## 🤖 Griha Nyay
**Aapka case short mein**
Aapka project 1355 days (~44.5 months) late chal raha hai.

**Aapki sthiti**
Kanoon aapka saath deta hai kyunki builder ne agreement ke mutabiq samay par kabza nahi diya. [RERA-18-1] "If the promoter fails to complete or is unable to give possession of an apartment, plot or building,- (a) in accordance with the terms of the agreement for sale or, as the case may be, duly completed by the date specified therein" aur [RERA-19-4] The allottee shall be entitled to claim the refund of amount paid along with interest at such rate as of interest and compensation ke tehat aapka yeh adhikar hai. Telangana mein byaj ki dar [TS-R15] "highest Marginal Cost of Lending Rate of State Bank of India plus two per cent" hai.

**Aapke do vikalp**
(a) Project chhodkar refund aur byaj lena (plus compensation). (b) Project mein rehkar har mahine ki deri par byaj pana [RERA-18-1] "interest for every month of delay, till the handing over of the possession". Aapke goal ke mutabiq aapka paisa wapas chahiye, toh pehla vikalp (refund) theek hai. Diye gaye figures ke anusaar Rs 30,00,000 diye gaye hain, par refund ke byaj aur total ki rashi abhi unknown hai kyunki har payment ki tarikh ki detail nahi hai. Agar aap rukte hain toh abhi tak ka byaj Rs 12,19,500 hai aur har mahine Rs 27,375 judta hai.

**Aage kya karein**
1. Apni saari payment ki raseedein aur builder ke saath hui baat-cheet ka saboot jama karein.
2. TG-RERA portal par project ka registration check karein.
3. Builder ko ek likhit notice bhejein.
4. Shikayat darj karein jiske liye [TS-R34-1] "file a complaint with the Authority for any violation under the Act" ke tahat Form 'M' aur [TS-R35-1] "file a complaint with the adjudicating officer for interest and compensation" ke tahat Form 'N' ka istemal hota hai (iska sahi rasta ek lawyer ya RERA portal confirm kar sakta hai).

**Mujhe kya pakka nahi pata**
Main koi vakil nahi hoon aur yeh kanooni salah nahi hai. Humne yeh nahi maana ki aapne sabhi bhugtan samay par kiye hain. Authority hi ant mein yeh tay karegi ki aapko kitna byaj aur compensation milega.

**Asli vakil ko kab pakdein**
Jab aapko RERA mein shikayat daakhil karni ho ya builder ke khilaf kanooni karwayi shuru karni ho, tab ek anubhavi vakil ki madad zaroor lein.
