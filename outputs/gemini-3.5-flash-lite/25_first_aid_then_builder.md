Separate problem: my builder Orion Developers promised my flat in Gachibowli, Hyderabad by 31 March 2024. I paid 48 lakh and still have no flat. I want the flat with interest for the delay.

## 🤖 Griha Nyay
**Your case in short**
Your possession is delayed by 959 days (~31.5 months) past the promised date of 31 March 2024.

**Where you stand**
The law supports your claim for delay. Under [RERA-18-1] "interest for every month of delay", if you stay in the project, you are entitled to interest until handover. In Telangana, under [TS-R15] "highest Marginal Cost of Lending Rate of State Bank of India plus two per cent", the calculated interest rate is 10.95% per year.

**Your two choices**
(a) Withdraw from the project and get your money back with interest and compensation; the exact refund total so far is unknown because specific payment dates are missing. (b) Stay in the project and get interest for every month of delay until handover. Since you want to keep the flat, choice (b) fits your goal. By my calculation, your monthly interest is Rs 43,800, and interest accrued so far is Rs 13,80,960 on the amount of Rs 48,00,000 you paid.

**What to do next**
1. Collect your proof, including payment receipts and your agreement for sale.
2. Check the project's registration on the official TG-RERA portal.
3. Send a dated written notice to Orion Developers demanding your delay interest and keep proof of delivery.
4. File a complaint if they do not comply, as [FAQ-8] states delay interest should be paid automatically under the agreement. Under [TS-R34-1] "Any aggrieved person may file a complaint with the Authority", complaints are submitted in Form 'M' with a fee of Rs 1,000.

**What I'm not sure about**
I am not a lawyer. I do not know if Orion Developers has registered this project, as the RERA registration number is missing. My calculation assumes all payments were made before the promised date and uses a simple interest estimate. The authority decides the final rate, period, and compensation.

**When to get a real lawyer**
Consult a real lawyer or free legal aid to review your documents and guide you before sending legal notices or filing formal complaints.
