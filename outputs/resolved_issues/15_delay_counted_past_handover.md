## 🤖 Griha Nyay
**Your case in short**
You took possession on 1 August 2025. Your bathroom ceiling leaks and the plaster is cracking. You want the builder to fix these defects.

**Where you stand**
You are within the 5-year window from possession (it ends 1 August 2030). [RERA-14-3] "it shall be the duty of the promoter to rectify such defects without further charge, within thirty days". If the builder misses 30 days, you can claim compensation. [TS-AGMT-12] excludes some items from free repair, such as fittings with natural wear and tear and painting-type wear; whether your defects are excluded is for the authority to decide.

**What to do next**
1. Send the builder a dated, written notice listing every defect and keep proof of delivery. I can draft it.
2. Take dated photos and videos.
3. If not fixed within 30 days, claim compensation from the adjudicating officer in Form 'N' [TS-R35-1]. Confirm the route with TG-RERA or a lawyer.

**About compensation**
Compensation for unfixed defects is decided by the adjudicating officer. I cannot give a figure.

**What I'm not sure about**
You have not given the date you reported the defects, so I cannot tell if the builder's 30 days have passed. I also do not know if your defects fall under an exclusion.

**When to get a real lawyer**
I am not a lawyer. Speak to one before filing to confirm the correct forum and exclusions.

**Delayed possession**
Possession was due 31 December 2023 and handed over 1 August 2025 (1050 days, about 34.5 months). Under [RERA-18-1] you can either withdraw and get your money back with interest and compensation, or stay and get interest for every month of delay. [TS-R15] rate: "highest Marginal Cost of Lending Rate of State Bank of India plus two per cent". Calculator figures: interest rate 10.95% per year; monthly interest if you stay Rs 65,700; interest accrued so far Rs 22,68,000. Refund figures are unknown without payment dates.
