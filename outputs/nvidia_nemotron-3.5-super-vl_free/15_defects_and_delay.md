## 🤖 Griha Nyay
**Your case in short**
You took possession of your flat in Lake View Heights on 1 August 2025. The bathroom ceiling leaks and the plaster is cracking.

**Where you stand**
If you report a defect within five years of possession, the builder must fix it free within thirty days; if not, you can claim compensation. [RERA-14-3] "it shall be the duty of the promoter to rectify such defects without further charge, within thirty days"
The five-year window from your possession date ends on 1 August 2030, so you are inside it. You have not given a date for reporting the defects to the builder, so the thirty-day repair period has not started.
The Telangana model agreement excludes some items from free repair, such as equipment under a maker's guarantee, fittings with natural wear and tear, allowable structural deformations, and painting-type wear. [TS-AGMT-12]

**What to do next**
1. Send the builder a dated, written notice listing every defect, and keep proof of delivery. I can draft it.
2. Take dated photos or videos of the defects.
3. If the defects are not fixed within thirty days, file a claim in Form 'N' with the adjudicating officer. [TS-R35-1]

**About compensation**
Compensation is decided by the adjudicating officer based on the facts; I cannot give a figure. [RERA-71-1]

**What I'm not sure about**
I do not know the date you reported the defects to the builder, so I cannot say whether the builder's thirty days have passed. I also do not know whether your defects fall under an exclusion in your agreement.

**When to get a real lawyer**
A lawyer can confirm the correct complaint route and whether your specific defects are covered.

**Delayed possession**
Possession was promised by 31 December 2023 and handed over on 1 August 2025, a delay of 579 days (about 19 months). The delay-interest figures are Rs 65,700 per month and Rs 12,50,640 for the whole delay period. [TS-R15] A lawyer can confirm whether anything else applies.
