## 🤖 Griha Nyay
**Aapka case sankshipt mein**
Shree Balaji Developers ne aapko Miyapur ke Balaji Residency mein flat ke liye March 2023 mein possession dene ka kaha tha, lekin ab tak nahi diya. Aapne Rs 30,00,000 diye hain. Delay 1325 din (lagbhag 43.5 mahina) hai.

**Aap kahan khade hain**
RERA ke tahat, agar builder samay par possession nahi deta, toh aap paise wapas maang sakte hain ya har mahine ka interest le sakte hain. [RERA-18-1] "return the amount received by him... with interest at such rate as may be prescribed". Telangana mein yeh rate SBI ka sabse uchha MCLR plus 2% saalana hai. [TS-R15]

**Aapke do vikalp**
(a) Withdraw karein: Rs 30,00,000 wapas + interest + compensation. Refund interest ab tak ka hissa unknown hai kyunki mujhe aapke payment ki tareekhein nahi pata. Payment ki tareekhein dene se yeh nikala ja sakega.
(b) Ruk jayein: har mahine Rs 27,375 interest, ab tak ka kul Rs 11,92,500.
Kyunki aapka goal refund hai, vikalp (a) aapke liye sahi lagta hai. Agar aap confuse hain, toh apne aap se poochein: kya mujhe ab bhi yeh flat chahiye, aur kya main builder par bharosa kar sakta hoon ki woh pura karega?

**Agla kadam kya uthayein**
1. Saboot jama karein: booking receipt, payment ki receipts, builder ke saath likhpat mein baat-cheet, agreement ki copy (agar hai).
2. TG-RERA portal par check karein ki project registered hai ya nahi.
3. Builder ko legal notice bhejein (main draft kar sakta hoon).
4. TG-RERA Authority ke samne Form M mein complaint dalein (fees Rs 1,000). Compensation ke liye adjudicating officer ke samne Form N ho sakta hai (fees Rs 1,000). Vakil ya TG-RERA complaint page se route confirm kar lein.

**Mujhe jo baatein pakka nahi pata**
- Possession ki tareekh March 2023 ka aakhri din maani gayi hai kyunki sirf mahina diya gaya tha.
- Aapne kab-kab kitna paisa diya, yeh nahi diya.
- Flat ka RERA number, agreement ki tareekh, aur builder ka koi excuse nahi diya.
- Antim interest, compensation aur period TG-RERA ka order tay karega.

**Asli vakil kab milein**
Complaint file karne se pehle ya agar builder notice ka jawab na de, toh vakil se salah lein. Main vakil nahi hoon.
