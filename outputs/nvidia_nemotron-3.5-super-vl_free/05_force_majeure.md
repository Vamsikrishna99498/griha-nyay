**2. Where you stand**
Your project is RERA-registered (TG RERA P02200009876). Under [RERA-18-1], if the promoter misses the possession date, you can withdraw and get a refund with interest plus compensation, or stay and get interest for every month of delay. Telangana interest is SBI MCLR + 2% per year (about 10.70% per year in 2026 orders) [TG-INTEREST]. Under [RERA-6], force majeure means events like war, flood, fire, cyclone or earthquake. Whether COVID or labour shortage qualifies is decided by TG-RERA on facts, and only if an extension is recorded on the portal. Check the TG-RERA portal for any extension.

**3. Your two choices**
(a) Withdraw: refund of Rs 55,00,000 with interest and compensation. Refund interest so far: unknown. Total so far: unknown. (Need payment dates for that estimate.)
(b) Stay: monthly interest Rs 49,042; interest accrued so far Rs 24,29,779.
Since your goal is possession with interest, choice (b) fits. If unsure, ask yourself: do I still want this flat, and do I trust the builder to finish it?

**4. What to do next**
