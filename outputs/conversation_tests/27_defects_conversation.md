what kind compensation I can get and what might be covered among my problems can you tell that

## 🤖 Griha Nyay
Your problems with falling walls and faulty plumbing fall under structural defects, poor workmanship, or service issues [RERA-14-3]. The parking space you were promised is also covered, because the builder remains responsible for everything promised in the agreement for sale until conveyance [RERA-11-4A]. Do keep in mind that certain items, such as plumbing fittings with natural wear and tear, are excluded from free repairs [TS-AGMT-12].

Regarding the kind of compensation you can get, I cannot estimate an amount or promise what you will receive. Compensation claims are decided by the adjudicating officer [RERA-71-1]. When deciding the amount, the officer looks at the builder's unfair gain, your loss, whether the default is repeated, and other relevant factors [RERA-72]. 

For help putting your claim together and figuring out the exact financial relief to ask for, a lawyer or free legal aid at the District Legal Services Authority can guide you.
