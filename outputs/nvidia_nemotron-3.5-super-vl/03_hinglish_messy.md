## 🤖 Griha Nyay
**Aapke case ka saransh**
Shree Balaji Developers ka Miyapur wala project “Balaji Residency” mein aapne 2020 mein flat book kiya tha. Possession March 2023 tak milna tha, ab tak nahi mila. Aapne Rs 30,00,000 diye hain. 15 November 2026 tak delay 1325 din (lagbhag 43.5 mahina) hai.

**Aapki sthiti**
Haan, law aapke side mein hai. Builder possession mein late hai.
[RERA-18-1] “interest for every month of delay, till the handing over of the possession”
[RERA-19-4] “refund of amount paid along with interest at such rate as may be prescribed and compensation”

**Aapke do vikalp**
(a) Withdraw: paisa wapas maangein, interest aur compensation ke saath. Refund interest ab tak ka figure unknown hai; iske liye har payment ki date chahiye. Total bhi unknown hai.
(b) Stay: flat waise hi lein, har mahine ka delay interest lein. Mahine ka interest: Rs 27,375. Ab tak ka interest: Rs 11,92,500.
Aapka goal refund hai, isliye vikalp (a) zyada fit baithta hai. Nahi sure hain? Pooche apne aap se: mujhe ab bhi ye flat chahiye, aur kya main builder par bharosa kar sakta hoon ki woh pura karega?

**Agla kya karein**
1. Proof jama karein: agreement, payment receipts, builder ke messages jahan woh “next month” kehte hain. (Ab tak koi evidence nahi diya hai aapne.)
2. TG-RERA portal par project ki registration check karein.
3. Legal notice bhejein (main draft kar sakta hoon).
4. Complaint file karein. Refund/delay interest ke liye Form M (fees Rs 1,000) aur compensation ke liye Form N (fees Rs 1,000). Sahi route vakil ya TG-RERA page se confirm karein.

**Mujhe kis baat par yaqeen nahi hai**
- Possession date 31 March 2023 se gini hai kyunki sirf month diya tha (case file ka assumption).
- Agreement date, total price, exact payment dates, RERA number nahi mile hain.
- Final interest rate, period aur compensation TG-RERA order se decide hoga, main estimate nahi kar sakta.

**Asli vakil kab milein**
Notice bhejne ya complaint file karne se pehle ek vakil se milein. Main vakil nahi hoon.
