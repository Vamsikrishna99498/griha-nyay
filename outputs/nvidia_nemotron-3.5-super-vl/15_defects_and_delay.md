## 🤖 Griha Nyay
**Your case in short**
You took possession on 1 August 2025. Your bathroom ceiling leaks and the plaster is cracking.

**Where you stand**
If you report defects within five years of possession, the builder must fix them free within 30 days. [RERA-14-3] "it shall be the duty of the promoter to rectify such defects without further charge, within thirty days". Your five-year window ends on 1 August 2030, so you are inside it. You have not reported the defects yet, so the builder's 30 days have not started. The Telangana model agreement excludes from free repair: equipment under the maker's guarantee, fittings with natural wear and tear, allowable structural deformations, and painting-type wear. [TS-AGMT-12]

**What to do next**
1. Send the builder a dated, written notice listing the bathroom ceiling leak and cracking plaster; keep proof of delivery. I can draft it.
2. Take dated photos or videos of the defects.
3. If the builder does not fix them within 30 days, file a claim in Form N with the adjudicating officer (fee Rs 1,000). [TS-R35-1]

**About compensation**
If the builder fails to fix the defects, you can claim compensation. [RERA-14-3] The amount is decided by the adjudicating officer; [RERA-72] covers this point.

**What I'm not sure about**
I was not given the date you reported the defects to the builder, so I cannot say whether the 30-day period has passed. I also do not know whether your defects fall under an exclusion in the model agreement. The case file lists no assumptions.

**When to get a real lawyer**
A lawyer can confirm the correct complaint route and whether any exclusion applies to your defects.

**Delayed possession**
Possession was promised for 31 December 2023 and handed over on 1 August 2025, a delay of 579 days (about 19 months). You can claim interest for every month of delay. [RERA-18-1] "interest for every month of delay, till the handing over of the possession". At 10.95% a year (highest SBI MCLR + 2%), [TS-R15] the interest works out to Rs 65,700 per month, or Rs 12,50,640 for the whole delay. This is a simple estimate; the TG-RERA order sets the final rate and period. A lawyer can confirm whether anything else applies.
