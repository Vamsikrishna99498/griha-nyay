**Your case in short**
Greenvale Homes promised possession of your flat at Greenvale Meadows, Tellapur, by 30 June 2024. As of 15 November 2026, the delay is 868 days (about 28.5 months). You have paid Rs 35,00,000.

**Where you stand**
The law supports your claim. [RERA-18-1] says the builder "shall be paid, by the promoter, interest for every month of delay, till the handing over of the possession." In Telangana, [TS-R15] sets the rate as "highest Marginal Cost of Lending Rate of State Bank of India plus two per cent."

**Your two choices**
(a) Withdraw: get your money back with interest and compensation. The refund interest and total are unknown because I need the date of each payment to calculate them.
(b) Stay: get interest for every month of delay. So far that is Rs 31,937 a month, totalling Rs 9,11,400 at 10.95% a year.
Since your goal is unsure, ask yourself: do you still want this flat, and do you trust the builder to finish it?

**What to do next**
1. Collect proof: agreement for sale, payment receipts, and any messages to the builder about possession.
2. Check the project's registration on the TG-RERA portal.
3. Send a legal notice (I can draft it).
4. File a complaint. A complaint to the Authority uses Form M with a Rs 1,000 fee [TS-R34-1]. A claim to the adjudicating officer uses Form N with a Rs 1,000 fee [TS-R35-1]. A lawyer or the TG-RERA page can confirm which route fits your case.

**What I'm not sure about**
The promised date is only a month, so I counted from 30 June 2024 as you assumed. I do not have your agreement date, total price, exact payment dates, RERA number, or whether the project is registered. The final interest period and compensation are decided by the authority.

**When to get a real lawyer**
Speak to a lawyer before you send a notice or file a complaint. Free legal aid may be available through DLSA if you qualify.
